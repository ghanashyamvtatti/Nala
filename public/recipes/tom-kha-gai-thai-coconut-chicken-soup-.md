# Tom Kha Gai (Thai Coconut Chicken Soup)

## Description
Beloved Thai soup with a unique balance of flavors: creamy coconut milk, savory fish sauce, sour lime juice, and the holy trinity of Thai aromatics.

## Ingredients
- Chicken Broth: 1.5 cups (unsalted is best so you can control the saltiness)
- Coconut Milk: 1 can (13.5 oz) — full-fat is best for creaminess
- Galangal: 1-inch piece, sliced into thin coins (about 10–12 slices)
- Lemongrass: 1 stalk (bottom half only), smashed with the back of a knife and cut into 2-inch pieces
- Makrut Lime Leaves: 5–6 leaves, torn in half (remove the center vein)
- Thai Chilies (Bird's Eye): 2–3 fresh chilies, smashed lightly (adjust for spice level)
- Chicken: 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces (thighs stay tender in soup)
- Mushrooms: 1 cup Oyster mushrooms or Straw mushrooms (button/cremini work too), torn or sliced
- Fish Sauce: 2–3 tablespoons (start with 2)
- Palm Sugar (or Brown Sugar): 1 teaspoon (optional, balances the salt)
- Lime Juice: 2–3 tablespoons (freshly squeezed is essential)
- Cilantro: Fresh
- Chili oil: Optional for color

## Steps
1. Infuse the Broth: In a medium pot, bring the chicken broth to a boil. Add the galangal, lemongrass, lime leaves, and chilies. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 5 minutes.
2. Cook the Chicken: Add the chicken pieces and the sugar to the simmering broth. Cook for about 5 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and turns white.
3. Add Coconut Milk & Mushrooms: Pour in the coconut milk and add the mushrooms. Turn the heat down to low. Simmer gently for 2–3 minutes until mushrooms are soft.
4. Season (The Taste Test): Add the fish sauce. Taste the broth. It should be savory and creamy.
5. Serve: Ladle into bowls. Garnish generously with fresh cilantro.

## Additional Information
To get the authentic flavor, you need these three fresh herbs. You generally do not eat them; they are there to infuse the broth (like bay leaves).
